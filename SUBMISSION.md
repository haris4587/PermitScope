# PermitScope — submission notes

Category: Projects.

PermitScope helps event organizers and permit issuers resolve disagreement about whether planned activities meet approved permit conditions. An issuer attests immutable permit text, dates, venue, capacity and restrictions; the organizer locks an event plan with hash-bound documents. Deterministic checks enforce measurable limits. Independent GenLayer validators interpret restrictions and exceptions, compare per-condition judgments and audit exact quotations from both documents. Outcomes are COMPLIANT, CONDITIONAL, VIOLATION or INCONCLUSIVE. The dashboard shows finalized receipts, effective status, challenges, reassessment, revocation and audit exports.

How to review: open the website and load DEMO-PLAN-001. Inspect locked documents and findings; use Register permit and Submit plan for new records. Browser wallets sign actual writes; Prepare in Studio exports exact inputs for signing with the Studio built-in account. Track the returned hash in the dashboard. See EVIDENCE.md for verified live actions and test results.

Trust limits: the app verifies documentary consistency, not legal permission or physical event safety. Issuer identity must be independently verified with the relevant authority. URLs are provenance references, not fetched authoritative facts. The on-chain walkthrough is explicitly synthetic. No government partner or real permit is claimed.
