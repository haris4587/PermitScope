What the platform verifies

PermitScope compares an issuer-attested permit with an organizer's immutable plan. It checks declared capacity, venue and UTC dates in code. GenLayer validators independently interpret every restriction, compare decision fields and audit quotations against both full documents.

What each result means

COMPLIANT
All declared checks and documented conditions are satisfied.
CONDITIONAL
Only explicitly noncritical conditions are violated. Resolution is still needed.
VIOLATION
A measurable limit or critical condition is violated.
INCONCLUSIVE
Evidence is incomplete, contradictory or ambiguous. Official review is required.

Trusted sources and honest limits

The registered issuer account attests the exact uploaded permit text. Verify its identity with the relevant authority outside this app. Source URLs are provenance references; the contract does not fetch them or certify government approval. Document hashes bind uploaded text, not an original PDF. AI assessments do not issue permits, establish legal compliance, or guarantee event safety.

Studio built-in wallet

Use Connect account → Prepare in Studio to download exact call inputs, then sign with GenLayer Studio’s built-in wallet. Paste the resulting transaction hash here to track consensus and refresh finalized records. Browser wallets can also sign through MetaMask when installed. Never enter a private key.

Public evidence

All submitted text is stored on a public test network. Use public permit records or synthetic documents, and remove personal data before submitting. Example fixtures are synthetic and make no claim of official approval.
